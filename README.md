# Assam Land Exchange (Prototype)

A verified land marketplace for Assam. Every listing passes six pre-flight checks
(ownership, lien, land class, protected belt or block, litigation, flood zone)
and officer approval before a buyer sees it as government verified.

Built for the North East Seva First Innovation Challenge 2026 (NESFIC 2026),
Problem NESFIC-D-52, Revenue & Disaster Management Department, Government of Assam.

**Live demo:** https://bigheaddotcom.github.io/assam-land-exchange/

## What the prototype shows
- Marketplace with filters, sorting and saved listings
- Pre-flight check score with a reason for each check
- Taxes and fees estimate, with TDS and receipt recording
- Deals tracker: offer, agreement, duties, registration, mutation
- Officer console and a hash-chained audit trail
- English and Assamese interface

## Important
All parcels, owners and results are invented sample data. There is no live records
integration. This is a proposal prototype, not an official government website, and it
supports, and does not replace, official registration and mutation.

## Status
Front-end prototype only. A backend, real data integrations and a pilot are proposed
in the NESFIC submission and are not built yet.
